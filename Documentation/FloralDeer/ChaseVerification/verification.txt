Floral deer attack-to-chase verification (2026-10-16)

Cause: Animator.speed scaled the whole fixed-time crossfade with navigation speed.
A 0.06-second transition took 0.61 seconds at Animator.speed 0.1, and could
stop progressing at zero speed. Small velocity changes also restarted Idle/Walk.

Fix: keep Animator.speed at 1 while entering Walk or blending, then scale
the established walk cycle to movement speed. Use separate start/stop speed
thresholds and clear navigation velocity when stopping after a charge.

  were Track/Walk with no transition pending. See after.csv.
